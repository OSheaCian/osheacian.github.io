From 2023 to 2025 I was employed as a Teaching Fellow in Economics at Trinity College Dublin. During that time I taught and developed a variety of undergraduate and postgraduate modules *(For more details on the each module click the link above)*.
- ECU33082 Mathematical Economics (3rd Year Undergraduate): **Lecturer**
- TEU00462 Sustainable Development Goals and Policy Evaluation (2nd Year Undergraduate Elective): **Lecturer**
- ECP77230 Research Methods for Economic Policy Analysis (MSc. Economics and MSc. in Economic Policy): **Lecturer**
- ECP77403 Introduction to Statistics and Regression Analysis (Diploma in Applied Economics and Big Data): **Creator and Lecturer**
- ECP77421 Microeconometrics (Diploma in Applied Economics and Big Data): **Creator and Lecturer**
- ECP77432 Macroeconometrics (Diploma in Applied Economics and Big Data): **Creator and Lecturer**
- ECP77172 Quantitative Methods for Economics I (1st Year MSc. in Economic Policy): **Lecturer**
- ECP77001 Econometrics I (MSc. Economics): **Lab Assistant**
- ECP77002 Econometrics II (MSc. Economics): **Lab Assistant**

## ECU33082 Mathematical Economics (3rd Year Undergraduate)
- **No of Years Taught:** 1
- **Role:** Module Leader/Lecturer
- **Topics Covered:** Differential and Difference Equations, Approximation Theory, Constrained Optimisation, Differentiation, Intertemporal Optimisation. 
- **Teaching Methods Employed:** In-person lectures and interactive tutorials. Additional video tutorials and question walkthroughs made available on VLE. 

## TEU00462 Sustainable Development Goals and Policy Evaluation (2nd Year Undergraduate Elective)
- **No of Years Taught:** 1
- **Role:** Module Leader/Lecturer
- **Topics Covered:** Introduction to development economics, Impact Evaluation Tools, Introduction to the sustainable development goals, and research related to specific goals.
- **Teaching Methods Employed:** Blended Delivery Module, Weekly Video Lectures and fortnightly in-person lectures, weekly activities on VLE (videos, readings and discussion board), and group presentation.
  
## ECP77230 Research Methods for Economic Policy Analysis (MSc. Economics and MSc. in Economic Policy)
- **No of Years Taught:** 2
- **Role:** Module Leader/Lecturer
- **Topics Covered:** Introduction to Research Methods, Formulating Research questions and ideas, Introduction to academic/research writing, Research presentations.
- **Teaching Methods Employed:** In-person interactive workshops, collaborative research groups, assignment-based assessment.

## ECP77403 Introduction to Statistics and Regression Analysis (Postgraduate Dip in Applied Economics and Big Data)
- **No of Years Taught:** 4
- **Role:** Lead TA, coordinated all teaching, taught weekly tutorial sessions, graded final exam.
- **Topics Covered:**  Introductory Probability and Statistics, Discrete and Continuous Distributions, Sampling Distributions, Estimation, Hypothesis Testing, Linear Regression.
- **Teaching Methods Employed:** In-person lectures and R labs, R video tutorials, homework, and practice assignments. 

## ECP77421 Microeconometrics (Postgraduate Dip in Applied Economics and Big Data)
- **No of Years Taught:** 1
- **Role:** Module Creator/Lecturer and Lab Assistant
- **Topics Covered:** Multiple Regression, Non-linear models, Introduction to Causality and Research Design, Panel Data, Difference-in-Difference, Regression Discontinuity Design.
- **Teaching Methods Employed:** In-person lectures and R labs, R video tutorials, homework, and research assignments.

## ECP77432 Macroeconometrics (Postgraduate Dip in Applied Economics and Big Data)
- **No of Years Taught:** 1
- **Role:** Module Creator/Lecturer and Lab Assistant
- **Topics Covered:** Time Series Analysis, ARIMA modelling, ARCH, Trends Cointegration, Causality in Time Series, Vector Auto-Regressive Models.
- **Teaching Methods Employed:** In-person lectures and STATA labs, STATA video tutorials, homework, problem sets, and applied research assignments.

## ECP77172 Quantitative Methods for Economics I (1st Year MSc. in Economic Policy)
- **No of Years Taught:** 1
- **Role:** Module Leader/Lecturer and Lab Assistant
- **Topics Covered:** Introductory Probability and Statistics, Discrete and Continuous Distributions, Sampling Distributions, Estimation, and Hypothesis Testing
- **Teaching Methods Employed:** In person lectures and labs, STATA video tutorials, homework, and practice assignments.

## ECP77001 Econometrics I (MSc. Economics)
- **No of Years Taught:** 1
- **Role:** Lab Assistant, delivered lab workshop syllabus based on the lecture material.
- **Topics Covered:** Cross-sectional and panel econometric methods, Regression, Logit, Probit, Difference-in Difference, RDD. 
- **Teaching Methods Employed:** Weekly STATA Lab Workshop, problem sets, econometrics project, readings and discussions.

## ECP77002 Econometrics II (MSc. Economics)
- **No of Years Taught:** 2
- **Role:** Lab Assistant, created lab workshop syllabus based on the lecture material.
- **Topics Covered:** Time Series Analysis, ARIMA modelling, Simultaneous Equations Models, Cointegration, Vector Auto-Regressive Models.
- **Teaching Methods Employed:** Weekly STATA Lab Workshop, problem sets, econometrics project, readings and discussions.
