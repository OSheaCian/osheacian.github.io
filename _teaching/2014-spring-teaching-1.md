From 2016 to 2023 I worked both part-time and full-time as a Teaching Assistant in Economics at the University of Limerick. During that time I taught a variety of undergraduate and postgraduate modules *(For more details on the each module click the link above.)*
- EC4904: Principles of Economics (1st Year Undergraduate): **Lecturer**
- EC4101 & EC4111: Microeconomics (Business and Non-business) (1st Year Undergraduate): **Teaching Assistant**
- EC4102 & EC4112: Macroeconomics (Business and Non-business) (1st Year Undergraduate): **Teaching Assistant**
- EC4004: Economics for Business (2nd Year Undergraduate): **Teaching Assistant**
- EC4404: Applied Economic Analysis (2nd Year Undergraduate): **Teaching Assistant**
- EC4307: Econometrics (3rd Year Undergraduate): **Lab Assistant**
- EC4427: Managerial Economics (4th Year Undergraduate): **Teaching Assistant**
- EC4018 & EC4418: Monetary Economics (4th Year Undergraduate): **Teaching Assistant**
- EC4408: Public Finance (4th Year Undergraduate): **Teaching Assistant**
- EC6061: Applied Data Analysis for Economics (Postgraduate): **Lab Assistant**
- EC6112: Econometrics (Postgraduate): **Lab Assistant**



## EC4904: Principles of Economics (1st Year Undergraduate)
- **No of Years Taught:** 1
- **Role:** Module Leader/Lecturer
- **Topics Covered:** Introductory Microeconomics & Macroeconomics, Demand and Supply Analysis, Elasticity, Utility, Consumer Choice, Producer Cost Theory, Market Structures and Analysis, GDP, Cost of Living, Growth and Productivity, Labour Market & Unemployment, Money & Banking, Income Expenditure Model.
- **Teaching Methods Employed:** Blended Delivery Module, Weekly Video Lectures and monthly in-person lectures, weekly activities on VLE (problem sets, tests, etc.), applied take-home final exam. 

## EC4101 & EC4111: Microeconomics (Business and Non-business) (1st Year Undergraduate)
- **No of Years Taught:** 6
- **Role:** Lead TA, coordinated all tutorial teaching on module, administration of over 600 students, teaching up to 10 tutorial sessions per week.
- **Topics Covered:** Introductory Microeconomics, Demand and Supply Analysis, Elasticity, Utility, Consumer Choice, Producer Cost Theory, Market Structures and Analysis.
- **Teaching Methods Employed:** Classroom discussion, group work, in-class assignments, examination preparation, open floor questions.

## EC4102 & EC4112: Macroeconomics (Business and Non-business) (1st Year Undergraduate)
- **No of Years Taught:** 6
- **Role:** Lead TA, coordinated all tutorial teaching and administration on module, taught weekly tutorials, and delivered online live sessions through Big Blue Button and MS Teams.
- **Topics Covered:** Introductory Macroeconomics, GDP, Cost of Living, Growth and Productivity, Labour Market & Unemployment, Money & Banking, Income Expenditure Model, IS – LM, AD – AS, Fiscal and Monetary Policy, The Phillips Curve.
- **Teaching Methods Employed:** Classroom discussion, weekly problem sets, group work, in-class assignments, examination preparation, open floor questions.

## EC4004: Economics for Business (2nd Year Undergraduate)
- **No of Years Taught:** 4
- **Role:** Lead TA, coordinated all teaching, taught weekly tutorial sessions, graded final exam.
- **Topics Covered:** Intermediate-level economics, Production Theory, Market Structures, Game Theory, Oligopoly, Risk and Uncertainty, National Accounts, Growth Theory, and Monetary Policy.
- **Teaching Methods Employed:** Case discussion, readings, group-work, in-class assignments, open floor questions.

## EC4404: Applied Economic Analysis (2nd Year Undergraduate)
- **No of Years Taught:** 2
- **Role:** Lead TA, coordinated all teaching, taught weekly tutorial sessions, and graded exams and projects.
- **Topics Covered:** Introduction to R, introductory and intermediate probability and statistics, basic data analysis, correlation, causation, and regression analysis.
- **Teaching Methods Employed:** Weekly tasks, Datacamp tutorials, research readings, group work, in-class discussion, open floor questions.

## EC4307: Econometrics (3rd Year Undergraduate)
- **No of Years Taught:** 3
- **Role:** Lead TA, created and facilitated lab workshops, assigned and supervised group assignments, grading.
- **Topics Covered:** Economic Modelling, Regression Analysis, OLS, Statistics, Classical Linear Regression, Homoscedasticity, Autocorrelation, Multicollinearity, Gauss-Markov Theorem, Hypothesis Testing, Multiple-Regression Analysis, Model Estimation, STATA, and R.
- **Teaching Methods Employed:** In-class workshops, applied data and programming instruction, facilitating group assignments and discussion.

## EC4427: Managerial Economics (4th Year Undergraduate)
- **No of Years Taught:** 4
- **Role:** Lead TA, created tutorial material, instructed tutorial sessions, facilitated group discussion, and graded assignments and final exams.
- **Topics Covered:** Advanced Microeconomics, Theory of the firm, Corporate Governance, Demand Analysis, Demand Estimation, Plant Optimisation, Make or Buy, and Production Analysis.
- **Teaching Methods Employed:** Case discussions, written group assignments, readings and discussions, provided additional video tutorials.

## EC4018 & EC4418: Monetary Economics (4th Year Undergraduate)
- **No of Years Taught:** 1
- **Role:** Lead TA, taught tutorial sessions, led group workshops, graded assignments and exams.
- **Topics Covered:** Wage & Price Setting Theory, Money, Banking and Financial Systems, Unemployment, Inflation, Technological Progress, Creative Destruction, Institutions, and Instability. 
- **Teaching Methods Employed:** Case discussions, applied data analysis project using R, discussion of applied research, and video-conferencing group work due to COVID-19.

## EC4408: Public Finance (4th Year Undergraduate)
- **No of Years Taught:** 2
- **Role:** Lead TA, taught tutorials, facilitated group projects, and graded assignments and exams.
- **Topics Covered:** Pareto Optimality, General Equilibrium, Social Welfare Functions,  Allocative Role of Government - Market Failures:  Public Goods, Externalities, Natural Monopolies, The Welfare State: Tax and Social Welfare Systems, Economics of Regulation.
- **Teaching Methods Employed:** Debt Sustainability data project, research readings and discussions, open floor questions.

## EC6061: Applied Data Analysis for Economics (Postgraduate)
- **No of Years Taught:** 2
- **Role:** Lead TA, taught weekly labs, created custom practice datasets, and do files for students.
- **Topics Covered:** Economic Modelling, Regression Analysis, OLS, Statistics, Classical Linear Regression, Gauss-Markov Theorem, Multiple-Regression Analysis, Model Estimation, Weighted Least Squares, STATA.
- **Teaching Methods Employed:** Weekly Lab workshops, additional video tutorial teaching theory and applied use of econometrics.

## EC6112: Econometrics (Postgraduate)
- **No of Years Taught:** 1
- **Role:** Lead TA, created lab workshop syllabus based on lecturer’s theoretical lectures.
- **Topics Covered:** Time Series Analysis, ARIMA modelling, Simultaneous Equations Models, Cointegration, GARCH Estimation, Vector Auto Regressive Models.
- **Teaching Methods Employed:** Weekly Lab Workshop, econometrics projects, readings and discussions.
